Josep Maria Comellas

Assistant Professor · Universitat Oberta de Catalunya (UOC)

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Law and Political Science at the Universitat Oberta de Catalunya (UOC). Previously, I was a Margarita Salas Fellow at the Democracy, Elections and Citizenship (DEC) research group in the Department of Political Science and Public Law at the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona (UAB). I received my PhD in Political and Social Sciences from the Universitat Pompeu Fabra (UPF) in 2022.

During my doctoral studies, I was a member of the Research and Expertise Centre for Survey Methodology (RECSM-UPF) and conducted a research stay at the University of Amsterdam.

My research focuses on public opinion and political behaviour, with a particular interest in political polarization and attitudes towards the European Union. My work has been published in international political science journals, including Political Studies, Electoral Studies, and South European Society and Politics.
